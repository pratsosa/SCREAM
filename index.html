SCREAM

Stream Characterization with Error-Aware Machine Learning

Alexandros Pratsos  ·  Biprateep Dey  ·  Ting Li

SCREAM is a research pipeline for detecting stellar streams in Gaia + DECaLS photometric data using a combination of normalizing flows and a Monte Carlo error-marginalizing neural classifier. The current application is the GD-1 stream.

Stellar streams are the remnants of tidally disrupted globular clusters or dwarf galaxies, stretched into thin arcs across the Milky Way halo. SCREAM targets the detection of stellar stream members in the presence of a dominant Galactic foreground using a data-driven approach that requires no simulation-based background model.
