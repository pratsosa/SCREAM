SCREAM

Stream Characterization with Error-Aware Machine Learning

Alexandros Pratsos  ·  Biprateep Dey  ·  Ting Li

Stellar streams are the remnants of tidally disrupted globular clusters or dwarf galaxies, stretched into thin arcs across the Milky Way halo. SCREAM is a pipeline for detecting stellar streams in Gaia + DESI Legacy Survey photometric data using a combination of normalizing flows and an error-aware neural network. Our current work applies SCREAM to the GD-1 stellar stream.
